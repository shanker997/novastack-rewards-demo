A few useful details.

Is this a live software service?

No. NovaStack and the activities on this page are fictional examples prepared for internal AppVentory Channels demonstrations. The sandbox does not connect to a real CRM.

What happens when I register or request discovery?

The workshop registration shows a local confirmation, and the readiness plan downloads directly from your browser. The discovery button opens SkyBridge’s tracked AppVentory Channels form; submitting that form creates a referral for NovaStack. Eligible campaign activity is attributed in the connected portals.

Does this book a meeting or take payment?

No. The workshop, sandbox, and plan download do not book meetings or take payments. Discovery requests are handled in AppVentory Channels and follow the account’s notification settings. Use fictional details and a new .example email address for every demo referral.

Why work with an implementation partner?

In this demonstration, NovaStack provides the software workflow and SkyBridge helps with discovery, data mapping, and the customer handoff. That gives the presenter a continuous story from first visit to an implementation opportunity.
